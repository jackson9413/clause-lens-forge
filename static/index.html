Clause Lens reads the document for the clauses that decide what happens when things go wrong: the liability cap, the termination notice, the renewal window, who owns the work, and what happens to the money when you leave early. It scores those clauses, shows you the wording that triggered each finding, and drafts the email asking for changes.

Not legal advice. It catches the clauses that are usually missing or lopsided; it does not know your jurisdiction or your leverage.
